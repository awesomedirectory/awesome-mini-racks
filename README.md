# Awesome Mini Racks [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of 10-inch mini racks for homelabs: frames you can buy, frames you can 3D print, planning tools, and the communities that build them.

A 10-inch rack is half the width of a standard 19-inch rack and uses the same 1U height (44.45 mm). That makes it small enough for a desk or shelf and still strict enough that parts from different makers fit together. The hard part is knowing, before you buy or print, whether your switch, mini PCs and shelves will actually fit the frame you picked. This list covers the frames, the tools for checking fit, and the places to ask.

## Planning Tools
*Check the fit before you spend a weekend printing.*

* [PrintableRacks](https://printableracks.com) - Free browser-based 3D planner for 10-inch racks. Pick a printable or ready-made frame, add gear from a catalog of about 700 parts, and it flags parts that will not fit (rail standard, U slots, width, depth, headroom above shelves). Outputs a list of what to print and what to buy.
* [RackArchitect MCP](https://github.com/itAlex240/rack-architect-mcp) - Open-source rack-as-code planner for 10-inch and 19-inch racks, with a clearance solver and parametric STL generation.

## Ready-Made Racks
*Metal frames and cabinets you can order.*

* [DeskPi RackMate series](https://deskpi.com) - Open-frame aluminium racks from 4U to 12U (T0, T1, T2) and the most common starting point for mini rack builds.
* [Delock 10" Network Cabinet](https://www.delock.com/produkt/43379/merkmale.html) - Enclosed, locking cabinets with a glass door in 4U, 6U and 8U.
* [MikroTik SolidRACK 5 mini](https://mikrotik.com/product/sr_5u_mini) - 5U open frame that can also mount under a desk.
* [Rack Magic Mini Rack](https://www.rack-magic.com/en/p/10-rack-3he-6he-9he-12he-tiefe-290-mm-wand-o-boden-montage-rack-magic) - Open wall or floor racks in 3U, 6U, 9U and 12U.
* [Triton RKA 10/19](https://triton-racks.com/products/data-cabinets/wall-mounted-cabinets/rka-10-19) - 10U wall cabinet, notable for being deeper than most 10-inch racks.
* [Waveshare HomeRack 8U](https://www.waveshare.com/homerack-8u-basic-kit.htm) - 8U aluminium rack with handles and feet.

## 3D Printable Rack Systems
*Whole frames and mounting systems, not single brackets.*

* [ButterflyRack](https://github.com/axiopaladin/ButterflyRack) - Modular printed frame designed around metal rails.
* [DeskPi 3D print models](https://github.com/DeskPi-Team/3DPrint-Models) - Official printable accessories for the RackMate racks.
* [HomeRacker](https://github.com/kellervater/homeracker) - Modular printable rack building system.
* [Lab Rax](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1294480-lab-rax-10-server-rack-5u#profileId-1325352) - Michael Klements' 10-inch rack system on MakerWorld.
* [LabStack mini](https://github.com/JaredC01/LabStack) - Printable faceplate and mount system for small devices.
* [Mod10](https://www.printables.com/model/1225275-modular-10-server-rack-mod10) - MandicReally's modular 10-inch rack on Printables.
* [Modular 10" Rack](https://www.printables.com/model/1170708-modular-10-rack) - bwees' fully printable rack.
* [PrintableRacks R-series](https://printableracks.com/racks/) - Five printable frame designs (R1 to R5) with downloadable print kits; can be planned in the PrintableRacks builder.
* [rackstack](https://github.com/jazwa/rackstack) - Modular, stackable printable mini rack system.

## Reference and Community
*Where the standard is written down and builds are shared.*

* [Project Mini Rack](https://mini-rack.jeffgeerling.com) - Jeff Geerling's reference for the 10-inch format: dimensions, compatible gear and a build showcase. The most complete list of mini rack hardware.
* [r/minilab](https://www.reddit.com/r/minilab) - Subreddit for compact homelabs, with or without a rack.
* [r/homelab](https://www.reddit.com/r/homelab) - The general homelab community.
* [10-inch vs 19-inch racks](https://printableracks.com/guides/10-inch-vs-19-inch-rack/) - Short guide to the difference in dimensions and what fits each.
